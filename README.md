# 📊 E-Commerce Sales & Revenue Analytics

## 🧠 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive analysis of e-commerce performance using Power BI.  
It includes dashboards for executive overview, sales trends, product performance, and geographic operations.

## 🧰 Tools Used
- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and data visualization  
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures  

## 📈 Dashboard Sections
### 1. Executive Overview
- Total Revenue, Profit, Orders, Average Order Value, Profit Margin  
- Revenue by Segment, Category, and Sales Channel  

### 2. Sales & Time Analysis
- Monthly revenue and profit trends  
- Year-to-year growth and performance metrics  

### 3. Product & Customer Analysis
- Top products and brands by profit and revenue  
- Customer segmentation and fastest-growing categories  

### 4. Geography & Operations Analysis
- Revenue by state and city  
- Orders by warehouse and shipping partner performance  

## 💡 Key Insights
- Technology and furniture categories drive the highest revenue.  
- Mumbai DC handles the majority of orders.  
- Profit margins remain steady at ~37%.  

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file.  
2. Open it in **Power BI Desktop**.  
3. Refresh data connections if needed.
